  1. On your other PC or Mac, install bekdesk (same installer) and open it.
  2. Make sure both are on the same Wi-Fi or network. If Windows asks, allow bekdesk on private networks.
  3. It appears here within a few seconds. Choose Share Input, Extend or Duplicate.

Tablet or phone? Install the free Moonlight app on it, open it on the same Wi-Fi and tap bekdesk. It shows up here to pair.
